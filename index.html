FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know. Still have questions? Hit us up on Discord or open a ticket.

What payment methods do you accept?
We accept PayPal, Stripe (Visa/Mastercard/Amex), bank transfer, Easypaisa, JazzCash, and cryptocurrency (BTC, ETH, USDT, USDC). Pakistani customers can pay via local bank transfer or mobile wallets — no international card needed.
Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan later?
Yes — upgrade or downgrade anytime from your dashboard. Upgrades are instant with zero downtime; you only pay the prorated difference. Downgrades take effect at your next billing cycle.
Do you offer a refund if I'm not satisfied?
100% yes. We offer a 7-day no-questions-asked money-back guarantee on every plan. Just open a support ticket within 7 days and we'll refund you in full — no forms, no hassle.
What's your actual uptime track record?
We maintain 99.99% uptime SLA backed by redundant infrastructure and automatic failover. Our public status page shows live uptime across all data centers. In the rare event we drop below SLA, you get automatic account credit.
Where exactly are your data centers?
7 locations: New York (US East), Los Angeles (US West), London (UK), Frankfurt (EU), Singapore (Asia), Dubai (Middle East), and Mumbai (South Asia). Choose during checkout for the lowest ping to your audience.
Do you support modded Minecraft? Which modpacks?
Yes! We support Forge, Fabric, Paper, Spigot, Bukkit, Purpur, and vanilla. Our one-click installer has 100+ modpacks: RLCraft, SkyFactory 4, All The Mods 9, Pixelmon, Better MC, Vault Hunters, and more. You can also upload custom modpacks.
Can I run multiple Discord bots on one plan?
Absolutely. There's no artificial limit on bot processes — you're only limited by your plan's RAM and CPU. Our Bot Pro ($6.99) and Enterprise ($13.99) plans use Docker for isolated environments, perfect for running 3-5 bots simultaneously.
Do you have a free trial?
We don't offer a free trial — but with our 7-day money-back guarantee, you risk nothing. Plus, plans start as low as $1.49/mo (Bot Basic) and $1.99/mo (Web Lite), so you can test the waters with minimal commitment.
Can I get a custom plan for my business?
Yes! For large-scale deployments, dedicated resources, or custom requirements, reach out to our sales team. We offer volume discounts, custom hardware configurations, and dedicated account management for enterprise clients.
